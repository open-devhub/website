[DevHub](/join) is a community, not a product. We don't have a business model that depends on your data, and we don't sell or share it with third parties. This document explains what we do collect and why.

## What We Collect

- Discord user IDs and usernames, necessary to identify accounts.
- Moderation logs, warnings, mutes, and bans, stored by Discord and our moderation bot.
- Bot interaction data, commands you run via [Quill](/quill) or other bots (query text, response, timestamp). Used to improve bot responses.
- ModMail and tickets you open with the mod team, stored securely and only accessible to mods.

## What We Don't Collect

- Your email address.
- Your IP address (we have no server-side access to this).
- Payment information of any kind.
- Data from private messages between members.

## Third-Party Services

[DevHub](/join) runs on [Discord](https://discord.com). Discord's own privacy policy governs how Discord handles your data, we have no control over that. The [bots](/pages/bots) we use (Wick, Sapphire, etc.) have their own privacy policies. Bot interaction data is processed by those services' servers.

## Data Retention

Moderation logs are retained for as long as the server is active, to ensure continuity across mod team changes.

## Your Rights

- Request a copy of any data we hold about you.
- Request deletion of your data from our records unless the data is required for moderation purposes.
- Leave the server at any time.

To make a request, DM an admin or open a ticket. We'll respond within 24 hours.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the admin team or via tickets or email at [open-devhub@outlook.com](mailto:open-devhub@outlook.com).
